SwiftShip Logistics — Shipping Policy
Version 2.1 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026

==============================================================
SECTION 1: SHIPPING ZONES
==============================================================

SwiftShip operates across 6 domestic shipping zones determined by the
distance between the origin warehouse and the destination address.

Zone 1: 0–50 miles from origin
Zone 2: 51–150 miles from origin
Zone 3: 151–300 miles from origin
Zone 4: 301–600 miles from origin
Zone 5: 601–1000 miles from origin
Zone 6: 1001+ miles from origin

Zone is calculated automatically at checkout based on origin ZIP and
destination ZIP using our proprietary Zone Mapping Engine (ZME v4).

==============================================================
SECTION 4: PROHIBITED ITEMS
==============================================================

The following items are strictly prohibited from SwiftShip services:

1. Hazardous Materials:
   - Flammable gases, liquids, or solids
   - Explosive materials and pyrotechnics
   - Radioactive materials
   - Toxic or infectious substances
   - Corrosive materials (acids, batteries leaking)

2. Controlled Substances:
   - Illegal narcotics and controlled drugs
   - Prescription medications without valid documentation
   - Tobacco products (certain state restrictions apply)

3. Restricted Items:
   - Firearms, ammunition, and weapons
   - Perishable foods without refrigerated service agreement
   - Live animals (except via SwiftShip Pet Program with prior approval)
   - Alcohol (without SwiftShip Alcohol Carrier License, by volume limits)
   - Currency, gemstones, and precious metals over $1,000 value without insurance

Shipments containing prohibited items will be rejected, seized, and
the shipper may be held liable for any resulting costs or legal action.

==============================================================
SECTION 5: INSURANCE AND DECLARED VALUE
==============================================================

All shipments include $100 base coverage at no additional cost.

Additional Coverage:
- $0.85 per $100 of declared value above $100 (up to $5,000)
- Coverage above $5,000 requires SwiftShip Extended Insurance Plan

Claims must be filed within 60 days of the estimated delivery date.
Evidence required: original invoice, photos of damage, packaging materials.

==============================================================
SECTION 6: PACKAGING REQUIREMENTS
==============================================================

Shippers are responsible for proper packaging. SwiftShip recommends:

- Use corrugated cardboard boxes rated for the item weight + 30% buffer
- Fill void space with packing peanuts, bubble wrap, or foam inserts
- Items should not shift more than 1 inch in any direction inside the box
- Double-wall boxes required for items over 20 lbs
- Fragile items must be labeled "Fragile — Handle with Care"
- Maximum package dimensions: 108" in combined length + girth

Improperly packaged items that are damaged in transit may have claims denied.

==============================================================
SECTION 7: ADDRESS CORRECTION AND RETURN POLICY
==============================================================

Address correction fee: $18.00 per package if correction is made after pickup.

Undeliverable packages will be returned to origin after 3 delivery attempts.
Return shipping fee: Standard Ground rate for the return zone.

Refused deliveries are treated as returns at the shipper's cost.

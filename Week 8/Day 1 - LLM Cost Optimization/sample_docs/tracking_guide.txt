SwiftShip Logistics — Shipment Tracking Guide
Version 3.0 | Updated: March 2026

==============================================================
SECTION 1: HOW TO TRACK YOUR SHIPMENT
==============================================================

There are four ways to track a SwiftShip shipment:

1. Online Portal (Recommended):
   Visit swiftship.com/track and enter your 18-digit tracking number.
   Updates are refreshed every 15 minutes.

2. Mobile App:
   Download SwiftShip Mobile (iOS / Android). Push notifications are
   sent at every major checkpoint automatically.

3. SMS Alerts:
   Text TRACK <tracking_number> to 79438 (SWIFT8).
   Carrier reply within 2 minutes with latest status.

4. API Integration:
   Enterprise customers can integrate tracking via the SwiftShip REST API.
   Endpoint: GET https://api.swiftship.com/v2/track/{tracking_number}
   Authentication: Bearer token (request at developer.swiftship.com).

==============================================================
SECTION 2: TRACKING STATUS CODES
==============================================================

Code        | Status Description
------------|-------------------------------------------------------------
ACCEPTED    | Package received at SwiftShip facility — processing begun
IN_TRANSIT  | Package is moving between facilities or out for delivery
OUT_FOR_DEL | Package is on the delivery vehicle for today's delivery
DELIVERED   | Package successfully delivered (timestamp and location logged)
ATTEMPTED   | Delivery attempted — recipient not available (see notes)
HELD        | Package held at facility (customs, address issue, or request)
RETURNED    | Package is being returned to the shipper
EXCEPTION   | Unexpected event — weather, damage, mis-sort (see notes)
LOST        | Package cannot be located — claim process initiated
CANCELLED   | Shipment cancelled before pickup

==============================================================
SECTION 3: UNDERSTANDING DELIVERY ATTEMPTS
==============================================================

SwiftShip makes up to 3 delivery attempts for packages requiring signature
or packages too large for the mailbox.

Attempt 1: Standard delivery window (8AM–8PM recipient local time).
           A door tag is left with the next attempt date.

Attempt 2: Rescheduled for next business day.
           A second door tag is left with a facility pickup option.

Attempt 3: Final attempt. If unsuccessful, an "ATTEMPTED - FINAL" notice
           is left and the package is held at the nearest SwiftShip facility
           for 7 calendar days before being returned to shipper.

Recipients can reschedule delivery or redirect to a SwiftShip Access Point
via swiftship.com/manage or by calling 1-800-SWIFT-01.

==============================================================
SECTION 4: DELAYS AND EXCEPTIONS
==============================================================

Common reasons for delays (EXCEPTION status):

1. Weather Events:
   Natural disasters, severe storms, or state of emergency declarations
   may pause delivery operations. SwiftShip issues Service Alerts
   on swiftship.com/alerts.

2. Address Issues:
   Incomplete, unverified, or incorrect addresses trigger a HELD status
   and an automatic email to the shipper requesting correction.
   Address corrections cost $18.00 per package.

3. Customs Delays (International):
   International shipments may be held by customs authorities.
   SwiftShip has no control over customs processing times.
   Estimated customs processing: 1–5 business days for most countries.
   Customs-required documentation must be complete and accurate.

4. Capacity Constraints:
   During peak periods (November–December), some services may experience
   1–2 business day additional delays. SwiftShip proactively communicates
   via email and SMS when peak surcharges or delays are expected.

5. Damaged in Transit:
   If a package is damaged during shipping, it is flagged with EXCEPTION
   and the recipient/shipper is notified immediately.
   A damage claim can be filed at swiftship.com/claims.

==============================================================
SECTION 5: LOST SHIPMENT PROCEDURE
==============================================================

A shipment is considered "officially lost" when:
- It has not shown a scan update for 5 business days (domestic)
- It has not shown a scan update for 15 business days (international)

Steps to file a lost shipment claim:

Step 1: Verify status at swiftship.com/track or call 1-800-SWIFT-01.

Step 2: File a trace request at swiftship.com/trace.
        SwiftShip conducts an internal trace for up to 8 business days.

Step 3: If not located, file a formal claim at swiftship.com/claims.
        Required: tracking number, shipper details, recipient details,
        item description, purchase invoice, declared value.

Step 4: Claims are reviewed within 5–10 business days.
        If approved, payment is issued within 15 business days.

Note: Claims for lost shipments must be filed within 60 days of
the original estimated delivery date.

==============================================================
SECTION 6: PROOF OF DELIVERY (POD)
==============================================================

Proof of Delivery is available for all delivered shipments:

- Digital POD: Available within 2 hours of delivery at swiftship.com/track.
  Includes: delivery timestamp, GPS coordinates, recipient signature
  (if required), and driver photo of placement.

- Printed POD: Available by request — call 1-800-SWIFT-01 or email
  support@swiftship.com. Standard processing: 1–2 business days.

- API POD: Enterprise customers can retrieve POD data via:
  GET https://api.swiftship.com/v2/pod/{tracking_number}

==============================================================
SECTION 7: SWIFTSHIP ACCESS POINTS
==============================================================

SwiftShip Access Points are partner retail locations (pharmacies, grocery
stores, service stations) that can:
- Accept package drop-offs for SwiftShip shipments
- Hold packages for recipient pickup (up to 7 days)
- Accept return packages

Find your nearest Access Point: swiftship.com/access-points

Packages held at Access Points beyond 7 days are returned to the shipper.
Identification is required to pick up packages at Access Points.

==============================================================
SECTION 8: INTERNATIONAL TRACKING
==============================================================

For international shipments, tracking updates may be less frequent once
the package enters the destination country's postal or courier network.

SwiftShip partners with 37 international carriers for last-mile delivery.
Once in-country, tracking may show on the partner carrier's site.

Customs documents required for most international shipments:
- Commercial Invoice (3 copies)
- Packing List
- Certificate of Origin (if required by destination country)
- Harmonized System (HS) Tariff Code for all items

SwiftShip Customer Support for International:
international@swiftship.com | Response time: within 1 business day.
